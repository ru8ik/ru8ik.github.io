Workshop Overview

Welcome to my digital workshop — a space that follows my journey in software testing and development.

This long-term project, which I'm creating to showcase my practical experience, also serves as a platform to develop and enhance my own skills.

Although it's still a work in progress and far from being complete, I thoroughly enjoy refining it bit by bit in my free time.

It's a simple static mini-website built with GitHub Pages, brought to life by combining a personal domain with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Technologies Used

  • GitHub Pages
